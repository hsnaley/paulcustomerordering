Interface options

Three directions for how the system looks and how it is laid out. Same screens and the same data in all three, so click in and use them properly rather than judging a picture. Whichever you pick becomes the look for the build.

Option 1

Pitch

The one you clicked through. Menu down the left, green and lime, a nod to grass and floodlights. It reads as its own internal tool rather than an extension of the public brand.

Open Option 1
My recommendation

Option 2

Matchday

Built around the board. The menu sits across the top rather than down the side, so all eight stages fit on one screen with no scrolling, and each stage carries its own colour from grey at New through to green at Complete. You can read where the work is piling up from across the room. Denser rows, squarer edges, your own black and blue with your logo on it.

Open Option 2

Option 3

Clubhouse

Built around calm. The menu shrinks to a row of icons and opens when you touch it, so almost the whole screen is your work. Roomier spacing, rounder cards, and headings set in a serif that nods to a matchday programme. The gentlest option for staff who are wary of a new system.

Open Option 3

Worth knowing before you choose. Nothing about how the system works changes between these. Same screens, same order stages, same data. What changes is where the menu lives, how much fits on a screen at once, and how it feels to sit in front of all day. None of it is locked in either, so it can still be adjusted once your staff have used it.

I would go with Option 2. Seeing the whole pipeline from New through to Complete without scrolling is the single most useful thing this system can do for you, and it puts your own brand in front of your staff every day. Pick Option 3 instead if the bigger worry is staff finding it daunting.
